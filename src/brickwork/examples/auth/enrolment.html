Renaming signup would not close this gap. HOW THE FLOW WORKS, matching app/onboarding.html: brickwork ships the progress indicator and nothing else. Each step is an ordinary Django page at its own URL. Each step POSTs to itself. On valid, your view saves and redirects to the next step. On invalid, it re-renders with the bound form. "Back" is a plain link to the previous URL. States: enrolment_state is the page's real branch. "ready": stepper + this step's form (bw_form renders valid/invalid inline). "empty": no enrolment session to resume (_empty_state). "error": the enrolment store failed (bw_alert danger). LOADING: not applicable for the default server-rendered path.
