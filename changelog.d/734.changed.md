- **Surface ambient shadow is a brand-overridable custom property**
  (icvoss/django-brickwork#734). Light surfaces pair `--bw-elevation-1` with
  `--bw-component-surface-ambient-shadow` (override to `none` for
  hairline-first brands). The property ships in the frontend mirror and
  component token source ahead of the next `@bwui/tokens` pin that publishes it.
