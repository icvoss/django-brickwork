- **`{% bw_theme_switch %}`, the live root-level axis control** (icvoss/django-brickwork#117,
  owner ruling 2026-08-07/2026-08-25). Every consumer wanting to demonstrate
  brickwork's live `color-mix()` re-theming (switch theme/density/direction
  and the whole page restyles with no rebuild) previously hand-rolled the
  control and its accessibility semantics; this ships a tested one plus the
  documented recipe (docs/BRANDING.md, "A live, on-page axis switch") for
  consumers who want their own. `axes="theme density dir"` by default
  (ADR-060 grammar); `brand` is opt-in only and requires `brands=`, since
  `data-bw-brand` means nothing without an authored `[data-bw-brand=...]`
  stylesheet. The no-JS floor is deliberately "render nothing" rather than a
  working control, since the server-rendered page is already correctly
  themed. Persistence follows the SHL-003 rule already shipped for the
  sidebar collapse: localStorage is the switch's own default, itself
  overridden per-axis by a real `BRICKWORK_THEME_RESOLVER` preference
  (surfaced as the new `bw_theme_locked_axes` context variable), so a client
  default never clobbers a server-resolved one.
