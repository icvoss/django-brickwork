- **`{% bw_ranked_list %}`, a ranked bar list** (icvoss/django-brickwork#183).
  An ordered list of label/value rows, each paired with a proportional
  decorative bar (a leaderboard or a top-N breakdown), rendered as an `<ol>`
  with visible label and value text and an `aria-hidden` bar rather than a
  per-row `role="progressbar"` (VIZ-015: the bar is one of an N-way
  comparison, not one quantity's progress toward a known target). Bar
  geometry (`basis="max"` default, or `"total"`) is computed in Python
  against a validated closed vocabulary, never built as a percentage string
  in the template; a zero or negative denominator degrades every bar to
  zero-width rather than raising. Rows require `label` and `amount`, and may
  carry a pre-formatted `value`, an `href` (the row becomes a real anchor),
  and consumer-owned `data-*` attributes. `loading=True` renders a skeleton
  row set; an empty `rows` composes `_empty_state.html` at `size="sm"` with
  the same `empty_heading`/`empty_body`/`empty_action_href`/
  `empty_action_label` passthrough `_data_table.html` already uses. New
  component tokens `--bw-component-ranked-list-bar-thickness` and
  `--bw-component-ranked-list-row-gap`.
